🧾 How it works

  • Private: what you type stays in this browser tab. This page never saves it, never sends it and never puts it in a share link. Spell check and autofill are off in the writing box, and the text is removed from the page when you shred it.
  • Two shred styles: strip-cut slices your note into 12 long strips; cross-cut also cuts across, into 120 small pieces. The shreds that land in the bin are blank paper.
  • The count: the only thing kept on this device is a number, how many notes you have shredded and how many today. Reset it anytime.
  • Sound: off unless you turn on Sound and vibration. If your device is set to reduce motion, the note is removed right away without the animation.

📚 Why write it down and throw it away?

In a 2024 study in Scientific Reports, researchers at Nagoya University in Japan asked people to write down their thoughts after they received an insulting comment. In two experiments (96 people in the final analysis), those who threw the paper in a trash can or put it through a shredder reported anger back at their starting level, while those who kept the paper stayed angrier than at the start. The study used paper, not a phone or computer, and measured anger right afterward; the authors note that digital versions have not been tested. This page offers the same simple idea, write it down and throw it away, without promising any result.

Source: Kanaya Y. and Kawai N., “Anger is eliminated with the disposal of a paper written because of provocation,” Scientific Reports, 2024. Checked September 2026.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is what I type saved or sent anywhere?

No. What you type stays in this browser tab. This page does not save it, send it or put it in a share link, and it is removed from the page when you shred it. Closing the tab also discards it. Only a count of how many notes you have shredded is kept on this device.

Does writing a worry down and shredding it help?

It may help some people, but this page is not a treatment. In a 2024 study in Scientific Reports, researchers at Nagoya University had people write down their thoughts after an insulting comment. Those who threw the paper away or shredded it reported anger back at their starting level, while those who kept it stayed angrier. The study used paper, not an app, so the effect of a digital version is not known.

What is the difference between strip-cut and cross-cut?

Strip-cut slices your note into 12 long strips. Cross-cut also cuts across, into 120 small pieces, like confetti. Either way it is an animation of your note, and the text is removed from the page when it finishes.

Can I turn the sound off?

Sound is off unless you turn on Sound and vibration. When it is on, you hear a short shredder sound, and phones that support it also vibrate briefly. The setting is remembered on this device.

What if worry or stress is hard to manage?

If worry or stress is affecting your sleep, work or daily life, consider talking with a doctor or a licensed mental health professional. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, which is free, confidential and available 24/7.
